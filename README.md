# Writing Samples — Fiscal Policy and Gender

Selected publications on gender-responsive tax policy and fiscal microsimulation,
produced during my time at the International Monetary Fund.

## Publications

### 1. Gendered Taxes: The Interaction of Tax Policy and Gender Equality
**Coelho, M., Davis, A., Klemm, A., and Osorio Buitron, C.**  
IMF Working Paper, 2022

Examines how the design of tax and benefit systems interacts with gender equality
outcomes. The paper develops a framework for identifying implicit biases in labor
taxation — particularly those arising from joint filing, secondary earner penalties,
and the treatment of childcare — and applies it across a range of country cases.

Presented at the National Tax Association Annual Conference under the session
"Distributional Effects of Tax and Transfer Policy."

[Full paper](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/WP/2022/English/wpiea2022026-print-pdf.ashx)

### 2. IMF TaxFit Model — Methodology and User Guidebook
**Cots-Capell, J., Davis, A., and Macdonald, D.**  
IMF Technical Notes and Manuals, 2025

Documents the methodology underpinning TaxFit, the IMF's gender-responsive fiscal
microsimulation model. TaxFit assesses gender-differentiated work incentives embedded
in tax and benefit structures across countries and household types. The guidebook
covers model architecture, data requirements, crosswalk construction, and
interpretation of outputs for policy use.

Funded by the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation.

[Full publication](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/TNM/Issues/2025/05/05/IMF-TaxFit-Model-Methodology-and-User-Guidebook561606)
